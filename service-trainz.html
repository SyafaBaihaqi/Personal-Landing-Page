What I Offer

  • Locomotive and rolling stock liveries (reskins) from your reference photos.
  • Custom models and assets built for Trainz.
  • Custom scripts (TrainzScript) and config.txt setup.
  • Preview images of the finished asset before delivery.

What You Get

Deliverables for the Trainz modding service
Deliverable Detail
Texture files Livery textures painted for the chosen locomotive or wagon.
Content package A .cdp file ready to import through Content Manager.
Script & config Custom TrainzScript and config.txt where needed.
Preview images Screenshots of the finished asset for your approval.
Install note A short note on importing the asset into the game.

How It Works

  1. Request Send your reference photos and tell me which asset to use.
  2. Confirm We agree on the scope, Trainz version, and deadline.
  3. Draft I send a preview of the asset for you to review.
  4. Revision Small revisions based on your feedback.
  5. Delivery Final .cdp package plus a short install note.

What I Need From You

  • Clear, high-resolution reference photos.
  • The exact locomotive or rolling stock the livery is for.
  • Your Trainz version (TANE, TRS19, or TRS22).
  • Any text, logo, or color notes to include.
  • A target deadline.

I am a beginner modder, so I take on small, well-defined projects. Pricing is discussed per project — message me on WhatsApp with your idea to get a quote.
